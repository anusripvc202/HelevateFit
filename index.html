End-to-End Care

A Seamless Journey from Lab Data to Daily Habit

From your initial blood panel and DEXA body composition scan to personalized nutrition pacing and clubhouse strength coaching, every step is coordinated by certified human performance coaches right inside your residential society.

Step 01

01 — Assess: Baseline Your Biology

We start with data, not guesswork. Complete comprehensive baseline diagnostics: metabolic health panel (ApoB, fasting insulin, hs-CRP), DEXA body composition, and optionally gut microbiome or DNA testing scheduled conveniently in your community.

Step 02

02 — Personalise: Build Around Your Data

Your certified coach interprets your Helevate Report and formulates a training and nutrition plan around what your data actually shows — translating numbers into daily priorities.

Step 03

03 — Train & Act: Execute In Your Community

Strength coaching, group fitness, and nutrition guidance delivered consistently inside your society clubhouse gym. No commute, no crowded gym floors, just focused coaching.

Step 04

04 — Reassess: 90-Day Objective Checkpoints

Repeat key markers and DEXA scans to objectively measure what has changed internally — not just how you feel.

Step 05

05 — Evolve: Continuous Adaptation

Your plan adjusts as your body changes. Escalate to medical referral or higher performance protocols as needed.

Biological Accuracy

Data-Driven Baselines. Zero Generic Guesswork.

Every human metabolism, muscle mass distribution, and lipid subfraction profile is distinct. Our diagnostic panels are chosen to provide the highest clinical signal for preventive longevity.

🔬

Metabolic Health Panel

Beyond routine bloodwork.

Routine labs can look “normal” while risk builds underneath. Our precision panel adds markers most routine check-ups skip — ApoB, Lp(a), fasting insulin/HOMA-IR, hs-CRP, and more — to reveal particle burden, insulin resistance, and inflammatory patterns early, while there’s still time to change direction.

Best for: Anyone 35+, anyone with a family history of heart disease or diabetes, and anyone whose weight isn't responding to normal effort.
Apolipoprotein B (ApoB) Fasting Insulin & HOMA-IR hs-CRP Inflammation Lipoprotein(a)
📊

DEXA Body Composition Scan

The real number behind the scale.

A single scan measures your actual fat mass, lean muscle mass, bone density, and visceral fat — the fat around your organs linked to metabolic risk. It’s the most accurate way to track real transformation, not just weight on a scale.

Best for: Anyone starting a fat-loss or strength program, and anyone who wants to track progress accurately.
Visceral Adipose Tissue (VAT) Regional Lean Mass Bone Mineral Density
🥗

Gut Microbiome Testing

Nutrition built around your biology.

Your gut bacteria influence digestion, inflammation, weight, and even mood. This test maps your gut microbiome so your nutrition plan works with your biology instead of against it.

Best for: Anyone with digestive issues, unexplained fatigue, or stalled progress despite a “clean” diet.
Microbiome Diversity Index Inflammatory Flora Ratios Short-Chain Fatty Acids
🧬

DNA / Gene Testing

Genetic insights into metabolism.

Genetic testing reveals how your body responds to different types of exercise, carbohydrates, fats, and recovery demands — turning your training and nutrition plan into something built specifically for you.

Best for: Anyone who wants a long-term, precision-built lifestyle plan.
Carb & Fat Sensitivity Recovery Latency Profile Nutrient Utilization
