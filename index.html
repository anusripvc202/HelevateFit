The Methodology

Measure → Understand → Personalise → Improve

We first understand you, then we understand what you need, and then Helevate provides the right health and lifestyle support.

01 MEASURE 🔬

1. MEASURE: Objective Baseline Diagnostics

We evaluate your metabolic blood chemistry, DEXA body composition, functional mobility, and daily recovery metrics to establish your true biological baseline.

02 UNDERSTAND 📊

2. UNDERSTAND: Identify Health Priorities

Our specialists interpret your biological data against your daily lifestyle demands, identifying underlying areas that require attention such as insulin sensitivity, posture stiffness, or sleep debt.

03 PERSONALISE 📋

3. PERSONALISE: The Helevate Report

All insights are synthesized into your personalized Helevate Report — a single, actionable health blueprint detailing your priorities across health, lifestyle, nutrition, and fitness.

04 IMPROVE 🌿

4. IMPROVE: Dedicated Support in Your Community

Certified coaches and health specialists work with you inside your society clubhouse, providing structured strength training, habit coaching, nutrition guidance, and 90-day reassessments.
