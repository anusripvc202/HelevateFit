Diagnostic Depth

Precision Health Assessments

Most health decisions are made with incomplete information. Helevate starts every client relationship with the right data — so your training and nutrition plan is built for your body, not a generic template.

Panel 01 • Biomarkers
🔬

Metabolic Health Panel

Beyond routine bloodwork.

Advanced lab panel adding key markers most check-ups skip — ApoB, Lp(a), fasting insulin/HOMA-IR, and hs-CRP — to reveal cardiovascular risk and insulin resistance early.

Best for: Anyone 35+, family history of heart disease/diabetes, or stalled weight loss.
Apolipoprotein B (ApoB) Fasting Insulin & HOMA-IR hs-CRP Inflammation Lipoprotein(a)
Panel 02 • Body Composition
📊

DEXA Body Composition Scan

The real number behind the scale.

Gold-standard imaging measuring actual fat mass, lean muscle distribution, bone mineral density, and visceral fat around vital organs with clinical accuracy.

Best for: Anyone starting a fat-loss/strength program who wants to track real physical progress.
Visceral Adipose Tissue (VAT) Regional Lean Mass Bone Mineral Density
Panel 03 • Microbiome
🥗

Gut Microbiome Testing

Nutrition built around your biology.

Maps bacterial diversity and inflammatory flora so your personalized nutrition plan works in harmony with your unique digestive biology.

Best for: Digestive issues, unexplained fatigue, or stalled results despite eating clean.
Microbiome Diversity Index Inflammatory Flora Ratios Short-Chain Fatty Acids
Panel 04 • Cardio & DNA
🧬

VO2 Max & DNA Testing

Cardiovascular & genetic metabolic insights.

Reveals genetic responses to carbohydrates, fats, workout styles, and aerobic capacity to build a tailored longevity blueprint.

Best for: Anyone seeking a lifetime, precision-built lifestyle and athletic plan.
