The Reality of Body Weight

The scale shows weight. It doesn’t show what you’re made of.

Standard scales measure total gravitational pull. They hide the critical changes occurring inside your body.

01. Muscle vs. Fat Confusion

A standard scale cannot tell if you lost 2 kg of valuable lean muscle or 2 kg of fat. Crash diets often destroy muscle, slowing your metabolism while the scale calls it progress.

02. Hidden Visceral Fat

The most dangerous fat surrounds your liver, pancreas, and heart. You can look relatively lean on the outside while carrying elevated visceral fat that stresses blood pressure and blood sugar.

03. Unguided Guesswork

Without objective baseline measurements, workouts in crowded gyms lead to frustration or joint strain. Real health transformations require knowing your numbers and training with coach eyes on every rep.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about coaching inside your community.

How long does a session take? +
Each session is 45 to 50 minutes of structured, coach-led training. With zero commute time and your coach ready when you walk into the clubhouse, workouts are concise and highly efficient.
Can people with injuries or past surgeries participate? +
Yes, provided your doctor has given exercise clearance. Coach Harish specializes in rehab-focused strength training, and with only two clients per slot, exercises are customized to protect your joints.
What happens when someone travels for work? +
Your coach provides custom travel routines via WhatsApp to keep your momentum going. Planned vacations can also pause your schedule with advance notice.
What food or nutrition guidance is included? +
Practical whole-food Indian meal frameworks, protein targets, and meal timing suggestions aligned with your Body Report metrics. We never prescribe crash starvation diets.
How is safety managed inside the clubhouse gym? +
Safety is central: strictly two clients per coaching slot. Your coach watches every repetition, manages weight loads, ensures clean movement patterns, and avoids dangerous maximal lifts.
What happens if a session is missed? +
You can coordinate with your coach to reschedule within the same week's available community slots or complete an alternate guided session via WhatsApp.
What is included in the Helevate Body Report? +
The Body Report includes a clinical DEXA scan measuring your exact body fat percentage, visceral fat level, lean muscle distribution, and bone density, followed by a 30-minute private consultation with your coach.
How do the 3-Day and 4-Day formats differ? +
Both formats feature the exact same coaching standards, Body Report measurements, and 2-clients-per-slot attention. The 4-Day format offers four coach-led sessions for faster adaptation, while the 3-Day format accommodates lighter schedules.

What to Expect & Preparation Guidelines

⏱️ How Long It Takes:
The DEXA scan itself takes only 6 to 8 minutes. Total appointment with the coach is approximately 30 minutes.
📋 What You Receive:
Your detailed clinical Body Report printout + a 30-minute 1-on-1 consultation with Head Coach Harish.
⚠️ Who Should Not Take a DEXA Scan:
DEXA uses low-dose X-ray technology. It is contraindicated during pregnancy. If pregnant or suspect pregnancy, do not scan; ask your doctor.
👟 What to Wear & How to Prepare:
Wear comfortable athletic clothing free of metal zippers, snaps, or underwires. Maintain normal hydration; light 2-hour fasting prior to appointment.
